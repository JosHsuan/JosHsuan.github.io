# Cinematic editing

Extend this role's camera responsibility to lens and editing decisions. A separate Editor role is unnecessary for a single continuous case: the same author decides framing, attention and the moment of a cut. Coordinate lighting with Lighting Designer, object motion with 3D Animation Designer, and the shared response clock with Motion Designer.

Read `research/round-02/OPTICS_EDIT_CONTRACT.md` from the Animation Cinematographer role root for the current case. From a repository-root session its path is `roles/animation-cinematographer/research/round-02/OPTICS_EDIT_CONTRACT.md`. The isolated launcher copies this skill, while the role research remains in the role workspace.

- Choose the photographic operation for the information: translate to reveal spatial relationships, change focal length for compression, rack focus between inspected depth planes, or conceal a short composition change with an authored veil. Do not apply every operation at every chapter.
- Receive the common damped `visualU` and remapped `stageU`. Never create a competing camera spring, independent wheel listener or automatic timeline. Native document progress remains immediate and controls semantic reading.
- Own a single final camera pose. Set aspect and film gauge before `setFocalLength`; do not then overwrite FOV. Focus distance is axial camera depth in model world units. Record sensor convention and avoid invented physical aperture or lens calibration claims.
- Depth of field must consume actual scene depth. CSS blur of the Canvas is a uniform image treatment, not a rack focus. Gather filters have foreground/background edge artifacts; inspect the real subject, not only uniform test cards.
- Keep hold compositions stable while a deliberately short focus or lighting envelope can transfer attention. Cut envelopes must seek and reverse deterministically. A pointer changes only the final small decorative offset, never shot selection, focus subject or source facts.
- Keep prose and documentary images sharp and directly readable in HTML. Reduced motion removes optical sweeps, cuts and defocus rather than slowing an unwanted transition.

Verify fixed-camera lens changes, fixed-pose near/far focus changes, entry/hold/exit and reverse scrubbing on the actual built case. Capture the same views with optical effects off to prove what changed. Check browser resize, depth near/far correctness, final color conversion, demand-loop settling and teardown. A passing mathematical check alone does not prove photographic quality.

This skill does not authorize publication, source-file mutation, fabricated Theatre exports or new facts about the work.
