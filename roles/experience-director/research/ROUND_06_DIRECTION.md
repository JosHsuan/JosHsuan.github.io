## Chapter score

Overview introduces material and scale. FORM compares folds and openings with a vector Miura diagram. SYSTEM relates the authored workflow to four actual source representations. PATTERN pairs the authored library with original shape experiments. MAKE and VALIDATION give photographs reading space; CREDITS closes the study with a quiet source-relative view. Each chapter has its own closed camera/light/focus rhythm and independent background-field time. Scroll selects a passage or accelerates playback rather than specifying a pose fraction. Deliberate manipulation holds the authored clock until the model returns; ordinary hover does not.

The background field uses coherent traveling contours, refreshed glyphs and pointer interference even where no geometry is present. Screen/soft-light/multiply-inspired mixes are bounded to the decorative composition. The final Canvas alpha is zero inside essential content cores, with rounded soft outer feathers. No HTML screenshot, extra Canvas or duplicate camera is used.

## Typography and interface

IBM Plex Sans is the primary variable family, Plex Mono carries indexes and captions, and Plex Serif Italic supplies selected closing lines. IBM describes these related families in its [typeface guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/typeface); the owner's explicit choice is the selection authority. The family remains under its SIL Open Font License.

Sans and Mono come from the previously pinned Google Fonts source revision `5e8a3ba899557829a76cfdac30fa512bda91d7ca` recorded in the UIUX asset catalog; FontTools converted format only, with no outline edits. Serif Italic and the license come from [IBM/plex revision 763c36ef9117782905ae010056dfbe8fd2653a25](https://github.com/IBM/plex/tree/763c36ef9117782905ae010056dfbe8fd2653a25). Exact public bytes and hashes are in the release manifest.

Rounded controls, nested corner radii, stable hit regions, layered inset rims and restrained perspective give the shadowboxes depth. These are project decisions informed by Apple's [motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) and [materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) guidance; Apple does not prescribe one universal radius. Photos use an orange inverse presentation, restoring the original on hover/focus and in the original-source reader. Scientific/vector colors are not subjected to that filter.

## Evidence boundaries

- Illustrator contains the editable diagram vectors; the inspected Photoshop document primarily contains raster/smart-object/photo adjustments. Full AI/PSD originals and native metadata are excluded from the public build.
- The four CAD representations share a verified source row. The color mesh retains its original colors. Its palette proportions differ from the printed reference; no palette reconstruction or Gaussian-value inference is claimed.
- Experiment names are editorial A/B/C because the CAD layers do not establish the printed classification. The printed type table is separate documentary evidence.
- The function library is an interactive reading of authored curves, not a new prediction function. Existing range/year/credit uncertainty remains unchanged.
- Preparation and private full-ID mapping remain in the designated D-drive workspace. The normal build consumes only explicitly allowlisted sanitized derivatives and license notices.
