# Round 03 — metal, light, exhibition and editorial response

Owner request: 8 October 2026, after the first public release. The owner explicitly requested stronger material appeal, deeper lighting/black-mist/transition research, permission for the subject to crop or leave the frame, richer information-layer feedback, free scene design, cross-role discussion, implementation, commits, push and deployment.

## Shared direction

Present the supplied object as a sculptural material study in a dark exhibition field. Broad warm/cool reflection bands explain its curvature; a directional satin finish replaces the busy chrome-like response. The existing source base is kept, while a clearly editorial graphite setting and light apertures establish depth. The setting is an authored presentation, not a reconstruction of the prototype site.

The seven factual chapters remain overview, form, system, pattern, make, validation and credits. FORM studies surface and opening; SYSTEM separates the verified display layers; PATTERN moves into detail; MAKE rejoins and reverses the composition; EVIDENCE gives documentary imagery priority; CREDITS resolves quietly. Camera cropping and temporary absence are deliberate shot categories. A full-object visibility assertion cannot apply indiscriminately to those shots.

The specialists exchange proposals directly before integration. Material/Lighting reconcile reflection width, HDR strength and highlight thresholds. Camera/Motion reconcile seven held poses with transitions and source absence. UIUX/Motion use the same playhead to separate and align objects while preserving the reading plane. Scene/Cinema keep the set behind the subject and test both landscape and portrait framing.

## Technical and verification contract

One persistent Canvas, one final camera writer and one native-to-visual response remain. Values are sampled into refs/uniforms, not per-frame React state. Motion must settle to idle. Reduced motion, Pause, Light, failed assets/WebGL and no-JavaScript reading remain useful. Source imagery and prose are never blurred by scene diffusion.

The public build continues to copy enumerated files and checksum-approved assets only. No dependency or original-source conversion is necessary. Verify actual shader compilation, highlight diffusion with controlled A/B pixels, stage/light influence, held and transition shots, reversal, idle, evidence controls, keyboard/focus return, mobile layout and fallbacks on the built public artifact. Browser emulation does not certify physical-device performance.
