## Transfer to this thesis — design decisions

Transfer the continuous spatial backdrop, readable foreground hierarchy and limited controls. Keep the real assembly large, stage it around the paragraph side, let documentary evidence pass through the composition and connect adjacent arguments with transitions. Preserve source imagery and legible body text instead of copying Labs' fading content, proprietary assets, fonts or ornamental treatment.

Native scrolling and decorative pointer tilt come from the owner. Use the thesis's graphite/off-white/orange identity and seven factual chapters without recreating the references' look or mechanics.

## Technical source and boundary

The MIT-licensed WebGL-Scroll-Sync example explains native-scroll/rAF timing mismatch for DOM-anchored WebGL objects. Its alternative scrolls an absolute Canvas and adds overdraw to reduce clipping. Our background assembly need not align precisely with DOM rectangles; a fixed Canvas is appropriate. Measure any later annotation-drift problem before adopting extra render area.
