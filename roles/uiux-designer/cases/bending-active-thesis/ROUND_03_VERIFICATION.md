## Cross-role review and resulting changes

The 3D Artist/Cinematographer, Lighting/Scene Designer and Motion/3D Animation Designer reviewed one shared implementation with the Experience Director/UIUX integration role. Material and lighting were reconciled against actual images, camera and motion against one response score, and interaction targets against native reading and keyboard behavior.

Two initial visual candidates were rejected: bright fragmented reflections with coarse grain, and a hard circular stage apron/horizon that competed with the work. The accepted revision uses broader satin reflections and one feathered graphite floor-to-wall sweep. A desktop SYSTEM collision with its evidence strip was corrected by raising and reducing the model; the same layout correction was then extended to the 1024px two-column view. Portrait layouts retain the model as a background layer behind prioritized evidence. The supplied mesh's real surface undulations remain visible.

Motion review moved animation into the inner contents of stable anchors and summaries, added separate FORM/SYSTEM/MAKE glyph behavior and a dark caption reading plate, and made the evidence dialog obey both Pause and live reduced motion. No essential prose is hidden by motion. Method disclosure text is grounded in the existing source narrative. The original UIUX Saved export is still unavailable; this does not claim to reproduce unobserved settings.
