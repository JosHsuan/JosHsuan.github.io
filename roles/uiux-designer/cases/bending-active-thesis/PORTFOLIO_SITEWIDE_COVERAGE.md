Bending adapters subsequently passed their separate acceptance and are included
in live 146e8938; the c6ede4 evidence above remains the generic milestone.
See [camera direction](SCROLL_CAMERA_DIRECTION.md).

A source-supported CPU baseline confirms generic models already fit eight exact
conservative source-bounds corners, prepared after streaming/validating original
vertices once. Node 24.19.0 warm batch-mean fit medians were approximately
0.36–0.45 microseconds across the five models; this is not GPU/frame-rate or
individual-tail latency evidence. No new geometry approximation is justified.
The candidate compares final composed camera values before fitting/drawing,
including inspection, and retains support only for that acquisition.
XR's full-source fitting is a different path requiring its own measurement.

The initial continuous 3D idle candidate failed browser throughput acceptance and
was not released. Its correction keeps genuine scroll/inspection camera changes,
but gives generic idle an explicitly **2D canvas pan/roll without parallax** on the
same clock; stable camera intent reuses the complete rendered source. Bending's
existing handheld recipe is unchanged. Bounded receipts require changing canvas
framing with no settled-idle WebGL draws/fits, fixed pixel/allocation budgets and
retained source framing. All five generic routes have accepted retained Chromium, WebKit and mobile
Chromium captures at that exact release. Settled-idle receipts showed eight CSS
updates with zero extra WebGL draws/fits. This does not establish physical-device
FPS or site-wide completion. XR now also uses its accepted separate 2D canvas
idle layer and exact prepared source support; Bending retains genuine 3D chapter
idle, its source-family timing and eight-active-second comparison policy. Both
are live at 146e8938, not interchangeable idle implementations.
