## Next shared rollout and release gates

1. Complete the bounded HC narrative candidate as a source-preserving slice;
   see [its evidence record](HC3DP_NARRATIVE_REFINEMENT.md). Its tests are not a
   substitute for the shared coverage matrix.
2. Replace the Mode-only eligibility decision with an explicit shared
   surface/route policy and a common transform contract. Review Home, About,
   Projects, Contact and all six generic cases together. Fixed chrome, source
   images and model interaction targets must not accidentally inherit text-plane
   transforms. Preserve focus, Pause, reduced motion, save-data, forced-colors,
   seek, visibility and resume behavior. Do not copy one amplitude everywhere.
3. In parallel with the shared motion policy, develop scroll-led authored camera
   paths and shots across the model-bearing routes. Click-and-hold rotation must
   not be the primary way to understand a model. Preserve the existing idle
   handheld micro-motion, continuous inter-section glide and single final camera
   writer. Do not merely remove controls and freeze the scene. Provide native
   keyboard/mobile reading and interruption/focus/reduced-motion behavior; any
   optional inspection must return cleanly to authored progression. Review
   native labels, hints and reset instructions with the input policy so obsolete
   "drag to inspect" instructions do not outlive their controls. Review
   Bending/XR adapter compatibility against their reference compositions and
   source geometry before changing their presentation. Route differences require
   an explicit rationale, not an untracked exemption from site-wide thinking.
   The current HC editorial slice intentionally leaves cameras unchanged until
   this dedicated camera rollout has its own validated replacement.
4. Continue the remaining evidence-backed narrative slices while checking the
   common opening, reading, source-link, transition and closing grammar. Source
   diagrams, fabrication footage and XR workflows need different explanations.

For every shared rollout, record route-level forward/reverse scroll, interruption,
focus/activation, native links, readable mobile states, no-JS/reduced-motion,
finite recovery and hidden/resume lifecycle. Retain actual screenshots/video
where the claim is visual. Check continuous model/camera ownership and source
bytes independently. Measure layout/paint/render cost before claiming a speedup;
software-browser CI is not physical-device FPS, real Safari or field INP proof.

The occasional XR non-finite camera result has not been reproduced with a proven
cause. The current diagnostic remains fail-closed and does not substitute a
plausible camera pose. A later passing run is not a fix. Investigate captured
numerical evidence if it recurs during necessary validation, rather than adding
unbounded full-CI retries or speculative visual/runtime workarounds.
