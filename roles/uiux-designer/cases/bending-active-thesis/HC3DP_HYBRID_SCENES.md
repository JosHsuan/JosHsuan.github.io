## Shared composition and protected behavior

- Desktop opening uses the existing complete-image and adjacent-glass columns. Mobile uses native image-first document order, followed by caption and original introduction.
- Results use the existing complete image → glass reading rail → evidence sequence. No image crop, filter, invented depth, new asset, duplicate inset or new camera move is introduced.
- Glass and velocity feedback remain under the existing owner. Native photographs do not register as fixed backdrop media.
- Context/System retain the original heavy model, authored camera, independent idle, inspection and Pause policies. Making retains its original video bytes, timing and transport; HC's looping simulation must not inherit Special Assemblies' non-looping policy.
- The approximately 50 MB model delivery cost is unchanged. Existing readiness deadline and fail-open behavior remain protected; this layout is not a compression or loading-performance fix. Delayed/failed model, reduced-motion, save-data and no-JavaScript reading require qualification rather than an assumed pass.
- Existing source geometry, source records, prose, credits and native full-image links remain authoritative. No per-frame React work, extra scheduler, added scroll pin or mobile spacer is needed.

## Scientific and visual boundaries

The photographs document observed forms and light patterns. They do not establish a quantified relation between a particular toolpath and optical performance. Sticking and overinflation remain visible limitations, not successful output relabelled for presentation. Original Group-Red attribution and individual contribution wording remain unchanged.

The opening photograph includes the hand-held light source and surrounding wall; retaining its complete composition avoids turning evidence into a misleading abstract effect. Existing photographic exposure, blur and resolution are source characteristics, not details this candidate reconstructs.

Results must preserve both limitation paragraphs and the orange sample caption. Long photographs and all prose need not fit simultaneously into one viewport; separate native reading positions must remain clear of fixed chrome.

Ownership checks must cover the original Context/System model and foreground plates, Making simulation, native Results, and reverse return to System without model/video carry-over into the photographs. Subsequent qualification must retain keyboard/source access, static/no-JavaScript and failure reading, reduced-motion/Pause behavior, native history, and other routes' unchanged ownership.

Static screenshots will not establish continuous motion quality, physical-device performance, exact optical accuracy or successful fabrication. No prototype, full-gate or publication result is claimed in this checkpoint.
