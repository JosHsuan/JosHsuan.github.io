## Scientific and visual boundaries

The photographs document observed forms and light patterns. They do not establish a quantified relation between a particular toolpath and optical performance. Sticking and overinflation remain visible limitations, not successful output relabelled for presentation. Original Group-Red attribution and individual contribution wording remain unchanged.

The opening photograph includes the hand-held light source and surrounding wall; retaining its complete composition avoids turning evidence into a misleading abstract effect. Existing photographic exposure, blur and resolution are source characteristics, not details this candidate reconstructs.
