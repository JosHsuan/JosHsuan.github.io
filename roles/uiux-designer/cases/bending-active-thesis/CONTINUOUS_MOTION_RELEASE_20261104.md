## Deliberate tradeoffs and limits

Bending's desktop scene uses a fixed 921,600-pixel ceiling, retaining 2× MSAA,
HDR optics and cached 1024-pixel shadows. This reduces raster detail on large
screens to keep continuous motion affordable. Native text and the separate XR
recording buffer retain their own resolution; touch stays at 520,000 pixels.
Project reading stages cache stable photographic lighting while compositor
handheld continues; Home retains authored light sweeps and real camera draws.
HC3DP uses a single-pass clear-shell alpha approximation with physical highlights,
filament normals and stronger edge coverage. It does not simulate optical
refraction or projected caustics; the real caustics remain documentary evidence.

The XR improvement recovers available original recording pixels without AI
upscaling. The composed phone is still bounded by its projected screen size;
it is not claimed to show every 1920 source row at every viewport. Apple/Lusion
informed the design language, not a claim of duplicating their renderer or full
Apple guideline conformance. The owner's always-motion instruction intentionally
differs from Apple's optional-motion recommendation.

The earlier failed qualification candidates remain failed and were not deployed.
Their retained investigations, including native hit testing, raster cost and
WebKit clock metadata and the unclassified native-target closure, remain in the
integration history. Added fallback/process observation is not represented as a
product fix for that closure. No failed artifact
or rerun-only acceptance was used for this release's full gate. The final
raster/clock correction retained its existing readiness and test deadlines.
