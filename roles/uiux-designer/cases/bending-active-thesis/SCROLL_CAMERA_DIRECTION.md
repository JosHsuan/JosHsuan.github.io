## Interaction and common grammar

Native vertical scrolling drives the main shot. Existing keyboard arrows are an
optional inspection layer; Home/Escape returns to the *current scroll view*, not
chapter zero. Help and accessible labels describe the actual implemented behavior.
Paused composition must stay fixed. Generic reduced-motion/save-data keeps the
existing latched original-poster/native-content policy and disposes live WebGL;
it does not introduce a static authored-camera mode. Normal idle must retain the separately owned
subtle handheld layer. Focus/inspection must not cause source clipping or surprise
camera movement. No new animation clock, source deformation or geometry changes.

Bending must adapt through its existing reading/camera score and retain source-fit,
chapter transitions and idle recipe; do not substitute scroll for active seconds.
XR must consume chapter progress in its camera pose and cache while preserving its
model/phone/workspace sources and recorded-video timing. Their obsolete drag help
must change alongside the actual adapters, not ahead of behavior.
