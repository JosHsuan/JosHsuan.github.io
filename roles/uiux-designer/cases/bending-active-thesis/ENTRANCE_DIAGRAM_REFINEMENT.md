21 October 2026. Candidate based on deployed `5b6997c`; not a release claim.

## Evidence and bounded change

The original raster plates were visually inspected at native resolution:
- `pattern-development.webp` (1107 × 783): four surface states, smooth form,
  triangular subdivision, depth, then openings with the original helical guide.
- `panel-system.webp` (1545 × 1300): Fig. 1-05 section, Fig. 1-06 three-joint
  assembly, Fig. 1-07 two-joint assembly, Fig. 1-08/09 folded panel families.
- `fabrication-drawings.webp` (1546 × 594): dimensioned drawings above, lettered
  a–f folding-angle details below. All numerical values and annotations retained.

Each Context/System/Making chapter now has a native radio-driven reading guide.
A newly authored outline selects one meaningful region of the COMPLETE raster.
It does not trace original vectors, crop, dim, mask or recompute source geometry.
A permanent link opens the complete pinned source. The native image alt, caption
and project credit remain present. Pixel bounds are authored attention regions,
not fabrication topology, measured dimensions or an assembly sequence.

Context prose introduces the four states before alternative patterns. System
connects the overall support section to the two named joint families. Making
connects panel drawings to their angle details and subsequent workshop evidence.
The guide is deliberate foreground support even where the optional background
model supersedes a primary plate; it must not disappear through source deduping.

Original source assets, media arrays, model/cameras/continuous interval, lighting
recording and all other projects remain byte/data-identical. No reconstruction
of the single merged mesh, invented joints, or physical-success claim is added.

## Interaction and lifetime

- Native radios offer sequential arrow-key selection and a Complete plate reset;
  no JavaScript/hydration is required for reading or selection.
- A 220 ms CSS outline reveal runs only after a discrete choice, with no timers,
  observers, animation frames, React state or new Director ownership.
- Existing `data-study-paused` and `prefers-reduced-motion` remove interpolation;
  selected regions and explanations remain immediately available.
- Source labels are never hidden; descriptions reserve their maximum height to
  avoid selection-induced layout jumps. The existing information presenter owns
  the enclosing glass surface. Print shows all descriptions without controls.
