## Design references

Apple's [Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)
describes material and motion as a connected system. Apply responsive rim light,
fluid recovery and restrained translucency; do not claim Apple's rendering is
reproduced. Content surfaces keep enough opacity for reading.

The official Pokémon [community introduction](https://community.pokemon.com/en-us/discussion/13247/letter-to-the-community-sept-19-2024)
and [collecting guide](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/strategy/a-guide-to-collecting-cards-and-using-wonder-picks-in-pokemon-trading-card-game-pocket)
establish layered cards, parallax depth and immersive reveals as references.
Use the interaction idea with this portfolio's own project imagery, neutral
glass palette and typography. Do not copy game assets, branding, purchase or
random-reward mechanics. All eight cards appear deterministically.
