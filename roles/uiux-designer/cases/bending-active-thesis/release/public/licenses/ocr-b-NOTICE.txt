Project derivative: Portfolio OCR B Regular, 2026-10-18.
All original OCR B glyph programs, mappings, metrics and original attribution
are retained. Twelve editorial/navigation characters are added using the
original dot, lowercase o, hyphen, plus and upward-arrow outlines: degree,
middle dot, multiplication sign, en/em dash, minus, ellipsis, and
left/right/bidirectional/diagonal arrows.
The credited name Jan Kyselý adds y with acute, composed from the unchanged
original y and acute outlines. No existing character mapping or metric changes.
No source character is retraced or replaced. The derivative is WOFF2 encoded
and renamed to identify this project adaptation; it is not represented as an
official Tsukurimashou release or certified machine-recognition font.
Project additions are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The original font notices above continue to apply to the original work.
Derivative 2.003 adds e with acute and e with grave for Disentis/Mustér and
Aurèle Gheyselinck, composed only from the original e, acute and grave outlines.
