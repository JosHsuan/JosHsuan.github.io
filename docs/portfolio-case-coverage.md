# Portfolio case coverage

Release record: September 30, 2026. The revised content contains **13 Work cases and 4 Lab studies**, with 62 narrative sections and 67 project images or figures. Twenty-three process figures were added to connect the methods described in the original material with the displayed results. The active records are in `src/content/portfolio.ts`; exact image files, PDF figures and crop bounds are registered in `docs/media-provenance.json`.

Later releases add two Work cases and expand the printing and motion coverage.
The current 15 Work / 4 Lab inventory contains 82 sections, 121 attributed media
figures and 28 playable clips. See [presentation coverage](presentation-supplement.md)
and [printing/motion coverage](motion-supplement.md) for the additional sources.

This record consolidates the source reviews for the retained 17 cases. Coverage means that each case preserves its supported context, methods, result, contribution and attribution at the depth available in its source. It does not mean that every archive file, image, working model, code module or historical version is included in the public website. Woodflow and ChinPaoSan / dome-tessellation were removed at the owner's request. The CV-only refinery project remains outside the inventory because a supporting case package has not been located.

## Excluded claims and interpretation limits

- **XR:** no measured acoustic/tracking accuracy, latency, frame rate, structural safety, usability or construction-speed improvement is established by this review. EchoXR's publication and the bamboo laboratory demonstrator support prototype outcomes; production deployment, delivered AR/BIM interoperability and a verified acoustic comparison are not asserted. Code demonstrates components and intended operations, not independent validation or sole authorship.
- **Fabrication:** metal prototype dimensions and the Heat fabrication time are source observations. The metal project's unsubstantiated one-hundredth transport-capacity comparison and broad environmental claims are omitted. Heat's heated finishing pass is not relabeled as a separate milling system. V-stick checks do not guarantee every possible assembly is collision-free. Non-planar printing's older ambiguous `TEPG` label is resolved by the later Group 1 presentation's explicit PETG spelling; its partially typeset point-count formula remains unquoted as executable code. Inside Out has no established material, sheet thickness, panel count or algorithmic winner.
- **Built team projects:** Caschlatsch study infill counts are not final as-built beam counts or performance results. Facade development does not confer authorship of the entire architecture. Nan Shan's source opening-height requirement of `200 meters` is an unresolved unit error and is omitted; 200 cm is not substituted. Jinshan's cost studies do not establish savings, and construction-framework photographs do not prove finished/accepted marble cladding. Its 3/5 mm colored warpage bands are analysis categories, not approved tolerances.
- **Generative/Lab:** Stare and Puff Waffle remain digital studies without fabricated or structural-performance claims. Planet's procedural variation does not establish biological simulation. Enter the Void has no verified lighting benchmark or product test. EggShell's continuous path expresses seam-avoidance intent without a measured thickness or guaranteed seam result. F5 participation does not imply authorship of the complete robot platform.
