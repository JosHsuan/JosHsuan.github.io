# Portfolio content revision

September 30, 2026. The owner requested removal of Woodflow and ChinPaoSan,
fuller case content based on the original portfolio, and factual research prose
instead of slogans. The retained website contains 13 main cases and four Lab
studies. The original archives remain read only.

## Editorial references

The following primary institutional pages were read for their narrative structure.
Their project facts and wording are not transferred into unrelated portfolio cases.

- [ITECH Research Pavilion 2024](https://www.itech.uni-stuttgart.de/itech-studio-projects/itech-research-pavilion-2024/)
  develops material context, system design, fabrication and the built demonstrator
  in separate sections. Process diagrams and photography explain those stages.
- [IntCDC research](https://www.intcdc.uni-stuttgart.de/research/) connects a defined
  research challenge to methods, projects and demonstrators that evaluate results.
- [ETH ITA: Anti-Ruin](https://ita.arch.ethz.ch/news-and-events/news-channel/2025/05/pietro-odaglia-on-the-anti-ruin-project-and-the-path-it-set-for-his-next-venture.html)
  names the material, manufacturing platform, development constraints and separate
  responsibilities of designers, technical developers and photographers.
- [Gramazio Kohler Research: Caschlatsch](https://gramaziokohler.arch.ethz.ch/web/lehre/e/0/0/0/496.html)
  explains site context, computational development and human-machine assembly,
  then gives institutional, participant and collaborator credits.

These observations inform an original case-writing method: state the problem,
describe specific design and fabrication operations, explain the physical or
digital result, and identify the author's contribution within the project team.
Section titles name their subject. Subtitles describe the project or method.
Home, About, Contact, Lab and metadata use factual descriptions and navigation
labels. Generic promotional statements are removed.

## Completeness and source treatment

Every retained case is compared to the relevant original portfolio chapter,
including diagrams and captions. The coverage record maps source pages and
meaningful technical details to the revised sections. Where a diagram explains
a mechanism or workflow absent from the first release, a focused source figure
is exported with its labels and attribution. The site pairs figures with the
sections they explain rather than assigning them by gallery position.

[Case coverage](portfolio-case-coverage.md) records all 17 cases and their physical
source pages. The revision contains 62 case sections and 23 additional method
figures, each with a full image, thumbnail and provenance entry.

Source-derived prototype dimensions and fabrication observations are identified
as study results, without generalizing them into production benchmarks. Missing
structural tests, comparative performance values, individual team responsibilities
and uncertain source labels are not invented. Conflicting archive dates are
recorded in the coverage notes; an index date is not treated as proof of continuous
development between two years.

Woodflow and ChinPaoSan are removed from active records, navigation, filters,
case generation, sitemap and dedicated public media. Their original archive
records remain available for historical source assessment. Retired public paths
must return the site's not-found response after deployment.

## Reading and validation

Long cases provide a contents list, readable paragraphs and explicit associations
between section text and figures. Image enlargement, credits, static HTML,
reading-position restoration and the reversible glass workspace are retained.
No background renderer or removed particle feature is introduced.

Validation covers the 17-case inventory, attributed media, section figure
references, retired path and asset removal, complete static paragraphs, local
resource links, TypeScript and the production build. Browser review checks the
expanded case layout, contents navigation, image dialog and narrow-screen reading.
Publication uses the existing GitHub Pages workflow, followed by live resource
comparison and retired-path checks.
